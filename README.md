# 📘 Engineering Leadership & Operations Playbook

Welcome to the **Engineering Leadership & Operations Playbook**. This repository is a curated collection of actionable frameworks, leadership models, and communication guides designed for engineering managers, delivery leads, and technical operations partners.

The goal of this playbook is to help technical leaders build high-trust engineering cultures, optimize team capacity, navigate change, and deliver complex technology products without unnecessary corporate friction.

---

## 📑 Playbook Modules

### 1. 💬 [Communication & Feedback Loops](./01-communication-and-feedback.md)
* **Active Listening & Perception Filters:** Navigating verbal, vocal, and body language cues to ensure intended meaning matches perceived meaning.
* **The Feedback Staircase:** Moving teams from defensive reactions (denial, argument) toward active listening, understanding, and growth.
* **Johari Window:** Expanding the "Open Area" through mutual feedback and active sharing to eliminate team blind spots.

### 2. 🎯 [Situational Leadership (SLII)](./02-situational-leadership.md)
* **Development Levels (D1–D4):** Assessing team members across competence, motivation, and self-reliance.
  * `D1`: Enthusiastic Beginner ➔ Needs **S1: Directive** leadership.
  * `D2`: Disillusioned Learner ➔ Needs **S2: Coaching/Explaining** leadership.
  * `D3`: Capable but Cautious ➔ Needs **S3: Supportive/Facilitating** leadership.
  * `D4`: Self-Reliant Achiever ➔ Needs **S4: Delegating** leadership.
* **Matching Leadership Style:** Balancing Directive vs. Supportive behaviors to accelerate skill growth and autonomy.

### 3. 🚀 [Building High-Performing Teams](./03-building-high-performing-teams.md)
* **The 5 Dysfunctions Model (Lencioni):** Overcoming absence of trust, fear of conflict, lack of commitment, avoidance of accountability, and inattention to results.
* **Trust Archetypes:** Moving from transaction-based and predictability-based trust to vulnerability-based trust.
* **Team Synergy:** *"Alone we go faster, together we go further."* Strategies for ensuring team performance is driven by shared commitment rather than isolated silos.

### 4. ⚔️ [Conflict Resolution & Alignment](./04-conflict-resolution.md)
* **Thomas-Kilmann Conflict Modes:** Balancing Assertiveness (concern for own agenda) vs. Cooperativeness (concern for others' agenda) across Competing, Collaborating, Compromising, Avoiding, and Accommodating styles.
* **The CUDSA Resolution Framework:**
  1. **C**onfront the conflict directly
  2. **U**nderstand each other's positions
  3. **D**efine the core problem[
  4. **S**earch for mutual solutions
  5. **A**gree on actionable outcomes

### 5. 🔄 [Change Management & Transformation](./05-change-management.md)
* **The Productivity Curve:** Guiding teams through Denial ➔ Resistance ➔ Exploration ➔ Commitment during platform or organizational changes.
* **Dave Ulrich’s 7-Factor Success Model:** Essential levers for leading change, mobilizing commitment, making changes stick, and monitoring progress.
* **Stakeholder Analysis Matrix:** Categorizing stakeholders across Agreement and Trust (Allies, Bedfellows, Opponents, Adversaries) to align leadership communication.

---

## 🛠️ How to Use This Playbook

- **For 1:1s & Coaching:** Use the *Situational Leadership* model to tailor your support based on an engineer's specific task maturity.
- **For Retrospectives & War Rooms:** Apply the *CUDSA Framework* and *Thomas-Kilmann Model* to resolve technical disagreements or cross-functional alignment blockers.
- **For Tech Transformation:** Use the *Change Journey* framework when rolling out new architectures, Cloud-Native practices, or AI integration models.
